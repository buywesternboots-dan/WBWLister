# WBW Lister Seller Guide

How to create a Western Boot World account, get approved to sell, and connect to WBW Lister.

**For:** sellers working with Dan / WBW Lister  
**Western Boot World:** [westernbootworld.com](https://www.westernbootworld.com/)  
**Intake sheet (Dan fills this on the phone):** [bootlister.vercel.app/intake](https://bootlister.vercel.app/intake)  
**Researched:** November 16, 2026, Eastern time, from the public site. Wording in quotation marks is copied from Western Boot World.

WBW Lister never signs in to Western Boot World with a seller’s password. There is no WBW seller API for us to use. We map the answers, you (or Dan with you) click the buttons on Western Boot World.

---

## 1. Create your Western Boot World account

Western Boot World has no separate signup page. Start on the homepage.

1. Open [westernbootworld.com](https://www.westernbootworld.com/).
2. Tap **Sign In/Register** at the top right (on a small screen it may say **Sign in**).
3. A pop-up opens, titled **Sign in to participate**. It says: “Want to sell? **You'll apply first**, a real person reviews every seller so the quality stays high.”
4. Tap **Create an account**. The title changes to **Create your account**.

**Choose one way to sign up:**

- **Google** or **Microsoft** — tap the button and finish on that provider’s page. After you return, Western Boot World may show **One quick confirmation**. Tick “I'm 18 or older and agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy,” then tap **Continue**.
- **Email** — under “or use email,” fill in:
  - **Email address**
  - **Password**
  - **Confirm password**
  - Password rules shown on the form: **At least 8 characters** and **A number**.
  - Tick: “I'm 18 or older and agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy.”
  - Tap **Create account**.
  - Check your email (and spam) for the verification link. You cannot sign in until you use it. If it never arrives, open the pop-up and use **Send the verification email again**.

You may also be asked to **Pick your handle** (your @username). It must be “3 to 20 letters, numbers, or underscores.” You can tap **Maybe later** and set it in your profile.

If you already have an account, tap **Sign In/Register** and sign in. Skip this section.

## 2. Get verified and approved to sell

Selling is application-only. Open your profile at [westernbootworld.com/me](https://www.westernbootworld.com/me), or sign in and open your account. (The site does not use a button labeled “My account.” Your profile tabs are **My Shop & Inventory**, **Watching & Saved Searches**, **Public Storefront Preview**, and **Settings**.)

On **My Shop & Inventory** you will see **Seller readiness**, in this order:

### 2.1 Complete Seller Profile & Location

Open **Settings**.

**Profile & Bio** — tap **Edit profile & bio**. Fill in:

- **Change profile picture**
- **Display name**
- **Username**
- **Aliases** (optional)
- **Location** (example placeholder: “City, State”)
- **Bio**
- **Fun fact about you**

Tap **Save changes**.

**Seller Policies & Public Contact/Payment Channels** — tap **Edit seller details**. Fill in:

- **Shipping & return policies** (one box, up to 400 characters; include how you ship, returns, and preferred payment)
- Optional: tick **Share my public contact channels**, then add **Facebook**, **Reddit**, **Phone**, and/or **Email** if you want buyers to see them

Tap **Save changes**.

### 2.2 Verify Phone Number

Tap **Verify phone**. The pop-up is titled **Verify your phone**. It says: “One verified phone per seller, this keeps the marketplace one-account-per-person. We'll text you a one-time code.”

1. Enter your **Mobile number**.
2. Use a real mobile phone. “Mobile numbers only, virtual/VOIP numbers aren't accepted.”
3. Tap **Send code**.
4. **Enter the 6-digit code**, then tap **Verify & continue**.

Western Boot World’s terms say: “Sellers must verify a real, personal phone number before posting listings.” You must be 18 or older, and only one account per person. The site does not ask for a government ID.

### 2.3 Seller Approval

Tap **Apply to sell**. The form says they “personally review your profile, usually within a day or two.”

Fill in:

- **Your name** (required)
- **Best email to reach you** (required)
- **Where do you sell boots today?** (required)
- **Link to your storefront or profile** (optional; leave it blank if you are just getting started)
- **What kind of boots do you deal in?**
- **Roughly how many pairs would you list?**
- **Anything else we should know?**

Tap **Send application**. You should see **You're in the queue**. They will email you when you are cleared. Status will show **Pending review**, then **Approved**.

You can keep browsing and buying while you wait. Listing and selling on Western Boot World are free: “no listing fees or commissions.”

### 2.4 Load & Publish Inventory

After you are approved and phone-verified, the badge is **✓ Verified Seller · Store Active**. Then you (or Dan, with you) can **Import CSV** or **+ Add a pair**. Nothing goes live until you choose to publish.

## 3. Connect to WBW Lister for import and ongoing sync

This is how it works today. We do not log into Western Boot World as you, and we do not have a WBW seller API.

### 3.1 Dan’s phone intake sheet

Dan fills the open form at [bootlister.vercel.app/intake](https://bootlister.vercel.app/intake) while talking with you. It is a form, not a seller sign-in.

When he saves it:

- A seller record is created in WBW Lister.
- A setup panel lists each answer next to the Western Boot World field it belongs in, with a copy button.
- Dan (or you, together) pastes those into Western Boot World and clicks every button there.

If you already have a Western Boot World account, say so on the form so we can link it. If you need a new account, Dan can pick a temporary password that meets WBW’s rule (at least 8 characters and a number), tell you what it is, and copy it into the **Create an account** pop-up. We do not use that password to sign in as you later.

### 3.2 Bring in your listings

Depending on where you sell today, we can import from:

- The **Chrome extension**, for Depop and Facebook Marketplace pages you are already using
- A **CSV / Excel** file you give us (or one you download from another marketplace)
- Other site or marketplace imports Dan already uses in WBW Lister

You choose whether those listings should go **live** on Western Boot World or sit as **drafts** you review first.

Western Boot World also has its own **Import CSV** tool and an optional **Link your eBay store** feature (approved eBay stores sync on WBW every two hours). That is WBW’s tool, not WBW Lister’s API.

### 3.3 Ongoing sync

WBW Lister watches the inventory tool you treat as primary and keeps an update list of what is new, sold, or changed. Dan uses that list to update Western Boot World.

**Plain fact:** Western Boot World has no seller inventory-sync API for WBW Lister. We cannot push changes into WBW automatically. Until WBW adds an official connection, updates are applied by hand from that list.

## 4. How we prepare your listings

**Photos: we optimize for everyone.** This is standard. It is not optional.

The next two items are separate. You can ask for either one, both, or neither. You do not turn them on yourself. A **WBW Lister admin** sets each one in the back end (Yes or No).

- **Extra description info: your choice (Yes/No).** Ask Dan if you want extra description text added. An admin turns this on or off in the WBW Lister back end.
- **Video: your choice (Yes/No), coming later.** Ask Dan if you want video. An admin turns this on or off in the WBW Lister back end. The video tool is still being built and will be applied later. Western Boot World allows 1 video per listing, up to 30 seconds and 100 MB.

---

## Quick checklist

1. Create (or sign in to) your Western Boot World account.
2. Confirm the verification email if you used email and password.
3. Fill **Profile & Bio** and **Seller Policies & Public Contact/Payment Channels**.
4. **Verify your phone** by SMS.
5. **Apply to sell**, then wait for the approval email.
6. Complete Dan’s intake sheet so WBW Lister has your details and the copy panel.
7. Import listings (extension, CSV, or another source). Tell Dan if you want extra description or video; an admin sets those. Photos are optimized for everyone.
8. Use the sync/update list to keep Western Boot World current. There is no automatic WBW API sync.

Questions: ask Dan, or email Western Boot World at support@westernbootworld.com.
